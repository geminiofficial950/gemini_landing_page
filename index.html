Questions, answered

Before you enquire.

CHC33021 is a nationally recognised Australian VET qualification when delivered and issued by a Registered Training Organisation that has the qualification on its scope of registration.

The qualification contains 15 units of competency: 9 core units and 6 elective units.

The qualification requires candidates to complete at least 120 hours of work as specified within the relevant assessment requirements.

The national qualification includes Ageing, Disability and combined specialisation structures. Availability depends on the training provider and elective combination.

Available delivery arrangements depend on the training provider. A Gemini adviser can explain current flexible options that may suit your schedule.

Gemini Education provides education guidance and enrolment support. The nationally recognised qualification is delivered, assessed and issued by the relevant Registered Training Organisation unless Gemini is expressly identified as the RTO for a particular offering.

No education provider or education agent should guarantee employment. Gemini can provide course and career guidance to help you prepare for opportunities in the sector.
